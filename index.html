Day by Day

Full Itinerary

1
Arrival — Singapore 🇸🇬
Wednesday, 29 July 2026
▾
  • Arrive Changi Airport at 09:40 — explore Jewel Shopping Mall and the iconic waterfall before leaving
  • Head to hotel 🚇 MRT Changi → City Hall → Bencoolen (~45 min, ~SGD 2) or 🚗 Grab ~SGD 20–25 if you have luggage
  • Drop bags at V Hotel Bencoolen — 7/11 is on the same block for a quick snack
  • TIP Request early check-in in advance (standard is 3pm)
  • Head to Gardens by the Bay 🚇 MRT Bencoolen → Bayfront (Downtown Line, direct, ~15 min)
    • Cloud Forest dome
    • Flower Dome
    • Supertree Grove FREE
    • Garden Rhapsody light show — 7:45pm & 8:45pm FREE
  • ⚠️ BOOK Dome tickets in advance: ticket2.gardensbythebay.com.sg
  • Dinner at Lau Pa Sat / Satay Street — short walk or Grab from Gardens (~10 min). End the night here
2
Little India · Kampong Glam · Marina Bay 🇸🇬
Thursday, 30 July 2026
▾
  • Breakfast near the hotel — cafes and kopitiams along Bencoolen Street
  • Little India 🚇 MRT Bencoolen → Little India (2 stops, ~5 min)
    • Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple
    • Tekka Centre
    • Walk through the colourful streets
  • Kampong Glam 🚶 10 min walk or 🚗 5 min Grab
    • Sultan Mosque
    • Arab Street
    • Haji Lane — especially lively in the evening
    • Coffee / dessert stop
  • Lunch in the Kampong Glam area — Arab Street has great halal options
  • OPT Head back to hotel for a rest, or continue to MBS Shoppes 🚇 MRT to Bayfront (~15 min)
  • Marina Bay — all walkable from MBS
    • Helix Bridge
    • The Merlion
    • The Esplanade
  • ⚠️ DECIDE Marina Bay Sands Skypark (paid, book ahead) OR rooftop bar — Spago / Lavo (drinks + food, similar views). Group vote needed
  • FREE Spectra Light & Water Show — 8pm or 9pm from the waterfront
  • End the night at Clarke Quay 🚗 Grab ~10 min or walk along the river (~20 min)
3
Chinatown · Orchard Road · Rooftop 🇸🇬
Friday, 31 July 2026
▾
  • Chinatown 🚇 MRT Bencoolen → Chinatown (Downtown Line, ~10 min)
    • Buddha Tooth Relic Temple
    • Chinatown Street Market
    • Maxwell Food Centre — lunch here
    • Optional: Chinatown Murders Game Tour (book via Klook)
  • Orchard Road 🚇 MRT Chinatown → Orchard (~10 min)
    • Takashimaya
    • ION Orchard
    • Paragon
    • Walk the boulevard
  • OPT Fort Canning Park — hilltop green space between Chinatown and Orchard Road, great for a short walk between the two. Easy to weave in without adding time pressure
  • OPT Haji Lane (evening) — if the day feels light, swing through Haji Lane before the rooftop. Best at night with fairy lights and cafés. A short Grab from Orchard (~10 min)
  • ⚠️ BOOK Rooftop for the evening — Friday night, fills fast. Options: Atlas · Ce La Vi · SKAI · MBS rooftop
  • ⚠️ EARLY NIGHT 07:55 flight tomorrow — be in bed by 10:30pm
4
Singapore → Perhentian Islands ✈️🏝️
Saturday, 1 August 2026
▾
  • ⚠️ Leave hotel by 05:00am — airport by 06:00 for 07:55 flight
  • 🚗 Grab to Changi Airport (~25–30 min, ~SGD 25–30) — MRT doesn't run this early
  • Fly Singapore → Kota Bharu · AirAsia · 07:55 → 12:05 (1 layover)
  • At Kota Bharu airport: pre-booked private van to Besut Jetty 🚐 Besutjetty.com
    • Cost: 360 RM round trip (up to 8 pax)
    • Speedboat to island: 78.4 RM pp round trip
  • Arrive Bubu Resort — check in, settle in, enjoy the sunset, eat, rest
5–7
Perhentian Islands — Free Days 🏝️
2 August – 4 August 2026
▾
  • TIP Book snorkeling trips the evening before at Bubu Resort's activity desk — August is peak season
  • Greater Island options: Turtle Beach · Teluk Pauh · Teluk Keke · Coral Beach (great sunsets)
  • Lesser Island (where you are): Mira Beach · Long Beach · Turtle Beach · Romantic Beach
  • Snorkeling / Island Hopping: Shark & Fish Point · Coral Garden · Turtle Point
  • TIP Turtle nesting season June–September — real chance of spotting sea turtles on the beach or while snorkeling
  • At night: fire show on Long Beach
  • Food options: Pitstop Cafe · Mama's Cafe · Panorama Kitchen · Ocean Boys Waffle · Mummy Burger · Roti Canai near fisherman's village
8
Perhentian Islands → Kuala Lumpur ✈️
Wednesday, 5 August 2026
▾
  • Wake up, breakfast, check-out by 11am
  • Speedboat from 12pm → mainland ~1pm · Pre-booked taxi to airport · Arrive ~2pm
  • ⚠️ Boat schedule is weather-dependent — pre-book return taxi to avoid waiting
  • Relax at lounge until 18:05 flight · Arrive KL 19:05
  • Hotel check-in, refresh
  • TBD Dinner near hotel — depends on hotel location. Options: Nasi Kandar Pelita · Halab KL · mamak nearby
  • OPT If energy remains: evening walk around KLCC Park or Bukit Bintang. If not — rest and start fresh tomorrow
9
Batu Caves · KLCC · Bukit Bintang 🇲🇾
Thursday, 6 August 2026
▾
  • Start at a mamak stall — teh tarik & roti canai. Leave hotel by 9:30am
    • 💡 Tamil Muslim-owned, always halal, cheap & open late
    • Search: "mamak stall near me" / "Indian Muslim restaurant" / "nasi kandar restaurant"
    • Options: Restoran Yusoof Dan Zakhir · Pelita Nasi Kandar · Restoran Insaf
  • Batu Caves 🚇 KTM Komuter — last stop on the line, arrive ~10:30am
    • Explore the caves and climb 272 steps
  • ⚠️ Dress code: shoulders & knees covered. Bring water. Watch for monkeys.
  • Head back to the city 🚇 KTM Komuter ~30–40 min → KL Sentral, arrive ~1:30pm
  • OPT Merdeka Square on foot — Sultan Abdul Samad Building · Dataran Merdeka · "I ❤️ KL" sign. Skip if running behind
  • Lunch at Kampung Baru 🚗 Grab — Malay hawker, almost entirely halal. Try nasi lemak · rendang · roti canai
  • ⚠️ DECIDE Petronas Twin Towers — pre-booked deck (~RM170/pp) OR KLCC Park only (free). Group vote needed
  • Walk through Suria KLCC mall — rest, coffee break
  • FREE Lake Symphony light show at KLCC Park — 7:30pm, right outside Suria KLCC. Petronas Towers as backdrop. Arrive by 7:15pm
  • 🚗 Grab ~10 min Dinner at Jalan Alor — KL's most famous food street
    • ⚠️ Not entirely halal — look for JAKIM certification or ask at each stall
  • End the night exploring Bukit Bintang (Jalan Alor is right in the heart of it):
    • Bintang Walk — pedestrian boulevard, lively at night
    • Pavilion KL — post-dinner dessert browse
    • Jalan Changkat — mocktail or dessert to close the night
10
Bukit Bintang · Jummah · Chinatown · Rooftop 🇲🇾
Friday, 7 August 2026
▾
  • TBD Breakfast — leave hotel by 9:30am. Options: VCR Cafe (western + local, near Bukit Bintang) · Oriental Kopi (kaya toast, nasi lemak) · mamak
  • Bukit Bintang — browse and shopping at Pavilion KL (Uniqlo here)
  • 🚗 Grab Head to Masjid Negara by 12:00pm for Jummah prayer (~12:30–1:15pm) — central and stunning
  • TBD Lunch after prayer. Options near Masjid Negara: Rebung by Chef Ismail (traditional Malay buffet) · Madam Kwan's (Malaysian classics, group-friendly)
  • Chinatown — Petaling Street 🚗 Grab — browse the market, souvenirs
    • Sri Mahamariamman Temple — Jalan Tun H.S. Lee (short walk from Petaling St)
    • Kwai Chai Hong / Mural Alley — Lorong Panggung, historic back lane with interactive murals
  • Central Market — Malaysian crafts and gifts (short walk from Chinatown)
  • ⚠️ DECIDE KL Tower — observation deck (~RM50/pp, views of Petronas Towers) or photo backdrop only. Best at 5–6pm
  • ⚠️ BOOK Sunset rooftop send-off — Vertigo at Banyan Tree KL (Level 59, views of both Petronas + KL Tower) · Friday night, book ahead
  • TBD Dinner — if rooftop is drinks only, options: Bijan Bar & Restaurant (upscale Malay, halal) · THIRTY8 at Grand Hyatt (Petronas views, premium)
11

  • Pack and check out — flight at 14:35
  • ⚠️ Leave hotel by 11:00am at the latest
  • 🚇 KLIA Ekspres KL Sentral → KLIA (~28 min, every 15–30 min, ~RM55/pp)
  • TIP If grabbing breakfast out, leave 45 min earlier and factor it in before heading to KL Sentral
